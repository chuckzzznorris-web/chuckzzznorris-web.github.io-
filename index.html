Our Services & Pricing

We handle everything: safe demolition, debris removal, site cleanup, and eco-friendly disposal (recycling metal/wood where possible).

Starting Prices for Empty Sheds

These are base rates for empty sheds (no contents inside) in good/average condition with easy access. Final pricing may vary based on shed material, foundation type, location challenges, or permits required.

  • Small Shed (up to approx. 8x10 ft or similar) — Starting at $250
  • Medium Shed (approx. 10x12 ft to 12x16 ft) — Starting at $350
  • Large Shed (over 12x16 ft, workshops, barns, etc.) — Starting at $500

Prices include demolition, haul-away, and basic site cleanup. Additional charges may apply for concrete foundations, difficult access, or special disposal needs.

Removal of Shed Contents

If your shed contains items (tools, furniture, junk, etc.), we can remove those too. Pricing is flexible and negotiated based on the volume, type, and weight of contents – often at very competitive rates. Just let us know what’s inside when you request a quote!
